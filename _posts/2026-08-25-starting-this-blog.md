I've wanted a place to write down what I'm learning for a while, mostly so I stop
re-deriving the same idea three months later. This is that place.

Expect short, informal notes on natural language processing, knowledge graphs, large
language models, and the occasional post about robots. No fixed schedule, I'll post
when I have something worth writing down.

If you want to reach me about any of it, the [contact page]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }})
has the usual links.
